Little Garden — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Introduction

Little Garden ("we", "our", "us") operates the Little Garden mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous Player ID: When you first launch the app, we assign a random identifier to sync your game progress across sessions and devices via Unity Cloud Save. This ID is not tied to your personal identity.
  • Game Progress: Your garden state, currency, items, and preferences are stored to preserve your progress.
  • Purchase Receipts: If you make in-app purchases, Apple (on iOS) or Google (on Android) provides us with a receipt to verify and grant the purchase. We do not receive your payment information.
  • Ad Interactions: If you watch rewarded ads, Unity LevelPlay (our ad provider) may collect data to serve and measure ads. On iOS, we show the App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads are personalized. You can opt out at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

How We Use Your Information

  • To save and restore your game progress
  • To grant in-app purchases
  • To display rewarded ads
  • To measure app performance and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

  • Unity Cloud Save (Unity Technologies) — for cloud save data
  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) — for rewarded ads
  • Apple App Store (Apple Inc.) — for iOS purchases
  • Google Play Store (Google LLC) — for Android purchases (future)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Data Retention

We retain your game data as long as your account exists. To delete your data, email gardentalent.contact@gmail.com with your request. We will delete your cloud save data within 30 days.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting gardentalent.contact@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Little Garden is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: gardentalent.contact@gmail.com
