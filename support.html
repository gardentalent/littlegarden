Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I plant flowers?
A: Tap an empty soil plot in your garden, then choose a crop from the menu. Once planted, you can harvest it for coins.
Q: My flowers aren't growing — what do I do?
A: Crops regrow after their timer runs out. If you don't want to wait, tap the growing plant and choose "Speed Up" to finish instantly with 1 gold.
Q: I lost my hearts. How do I get more?
A: Hearts refill every day at the daily reset. You can also watch a rewarded ad for extra hearts, or purchase heart packs from the in-game shop.
Q: How do I get free flowers?
A: Every day, pickup hand icons appear on some of your garden plots. Tap them to collect free flowers. You can pick up to 3 per day — one of the plots might give you a bird instead of a flower!
Q: I made an in-app purchase but didn't receive my hearts.
A: Please email us with your Apple receipt number (from the confirmation email Apple sent you) and we'll investigate right away.
Q: How do I reset my game progress?
A: Deleting the app and reinstalling will reset your local save. If your game is linked to cloud save, your progress will be restored on reinstall.
Q: The game crashed — what can I do?
A: Please email us with your device model (e.g., iPhone 16 Pro) and iOS version, and describe what happened. This helps us fix it quickly.
